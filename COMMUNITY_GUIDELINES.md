# Studable — Community Guidelines

**Our Mission:** To provide a safe, respectful, and constructive networking space where students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and mentors can connect and collaborate.

To ensure that Studable remains a positive community for everyone, all members are expected to uphold the following guidelines.

---

## 1. Core Principles

- **Be Respectful:** Treat every member with courtesy and empathy. Disagreements are natural, but personal attacks, insults, and belittling remarks are not tolerated.
- **Connect Authentically:** Represent your background, skills, and goals honestly. Authentic networking fosters meaningful collaboration.
- **Support Learning & Growth:** Encourage peers, share knowledge generously, and keep interactions constructive.

---

## 2. Zero-Tolerance Violations

The following behaviors will result in **immediate suspension or permanent banning**:
- **Harassment & Stalking:** Repeated unwanted messaging, persistent contact after being asked to stop, intimidation, or doxxing.
- **Hate Speech & Discrimination:** Content or language promoting violence, hatred, or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
- **Sexual Misconduct:** Explicit sexual advances, solicitation, sexually suggestive media, or non-consensual sexual discourse.
- **Scams & Fraud:** Financial solicitations, pyramid schemes, phishing, selling unauthorized products, or fraudulent claims.
- **Exploitation of Minors:** Any inappropriate communication or exploitation directed toward minors will result in immediate termination and referral to law enforcement.

---

## 3. Communication Etiquette

- **Respect Boundaries:** If a peer declines a match proposal or ends a conversation, respect their decision.
- **Keep Lobby Clean:** Do not spam the matching lobby or repeatedly join and cancel queues to disrupt pairing algorithms.
- **No Promotional Spam:** Studable is a networking platform, not a marketing board. Do not blast sales pitches or affiliate links.

---

## 4. Safety Tools

We provide tools directly within the application to safeguard your experience:
- **End Conversation:** You can disconnect from any 1-on-1 chat at any time.
- **Block:** Blocking a user immediately prevents them from matching with you, messaging you, or viewing your profile.
- **Report:** If someone violates these guidelines, use the **Report** button in the chat or user profile. Provide concise context to assist our moderation team.

---

## 5. How Reports Are Handled

1. **Submission:** When a report is filed, it enters our administrative moderation queue with contextual conversation metadata.
2. **Review:** A human moderator examines the report, the reported user's recent history, and the relevant interaction transcripts.
3. **Resolution:** Moderators can dismiss unsubstantiated reports, issue warnings, or suspend the offending account immediately.
4. **Appeals:** If you believe your account was suspended in error, you may contact `moderation@studable.app` for review.
